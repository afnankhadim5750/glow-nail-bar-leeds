About the salon

A calm chair, a careful finish

On The Parade in Headingley, Glow Nail Bar has built its name the slow way: one neat set at a time. Customers talk about friendly staff, a relaxed room, and nails that stay looking good long after the appointment.

Whether it is a classic manicure, a gel finish or a full set of extensions, the approach is the same — shape first, colour second, detail checked before you leave.
